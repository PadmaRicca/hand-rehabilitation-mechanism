**Project Type:** Individual SOLIDWORKS mechanical design and assembly project

## Project Overview

Independently designed a mechanical hand rehabilitation mechanism in SOLIDWORKS as part of an assessment evaluating computer-aided design (CAD) skills.

Although a biomedical application was not required, I chose to address the challenge of maintaining joint mobility in patients in a coma, who may require passive limb mobilisation due to prolonged immobility. The proposed mechanism was intended to explore mechanically assisted finger movement as an alternative to manually performed exercises.

The CAD assembly incorporated a hand-operated crank, cams, followers and finger supports to convert rotational motion into linear displacement.

## Contributions

- Independently conceived the biomedical application beyond the assessment requirements.
- Modelled individual mechanical components and developed the complete SOLIDWORKS assembly.
- Designed a cam-and-follower arrangement to explore rotational-to-linear motion conversion.
- Applied modelling features and assembly mating constraints to define component geometry and mechanical relationships.
